Privacy Policy for Dropers Delivery

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dropers Delivery ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using Dropers Delivery, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

To provide our delivery service, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: your name, phone number, and email address when you register or sign in.
  • Delivery information: pickup and drop-off addresses you provide for orders.
  • Location data: with your permission, we access your device's location to set your store pickup point and to support accurate delivery tracking.
  • Device and usage information: basic technical information such as device type and app usage, used to keep the app running reliably.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process and track deliveries
  • To set and confirm your store's pickup location
  • To send you notifications about your orders and account activity
  • To improve the reliability and performance of the app

Location Data

Dropers Delivery requests access to your device's location only while the app is in use. This is used specifically to help you set an accurate store pickup point and to support delivery coordination. You can disable location access at any time through your device settings, though some features of the app may not work correctly without it.

Notifications

We use push notifications to inform you about order updates, delivery status, and important account activity. You can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.

Payments

Dropers Delivery does not process or store any payment information within this app.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information as necessary to operate the delivery service — for example, sharing relevant order and location details between customers, drivers, and stores involved in a specific delivery — or where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your account and delivery information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Dropers Delivery is not intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
