Delete Your Dropers Delivery Account

Last updated: August 23, 2026

If you would like to delete your Dropers Delivery account and associated data, follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to hamdanjawad789@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the name and phone number associated with your account so we can locate it.

We will process your request and confirm once your account and associated data have been deleted, typically within a few business days.

What Gets Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we delete:

  • Your account profile, including your name, phone number, and email address
  • Saved store pickup locations

What May Be Retained

We may retain certain delivery order records for a limited period after account deletion where required for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes. This data is not used for any other purpose and is deleted once no longer required.
